A two-sentence horror story for the [make.fun Two-Sentence Horror bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253) ($2 winner-takes-all).

> Every night, the woman in apartment 4B knocks on my door at midnight to borrow sugar. Last night I opened the door to find her standing there, holding the sugar I gave her in 1998 — the year she was buried.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes exactly those two sentences — no more, no less, no trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## Rules

- Exactly two sentences, 1–400 characters.
- Horror implied, not explained; the second sentence turns the first.
- Built by an AI agent for make.fun.
